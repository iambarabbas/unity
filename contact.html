About Unity

Local, accountable, and invested in your returns.

Unity Property Management is a San Diego County firm built around a simple idea: a rental should be run with the same care an owner would give it — and the same attention to the numbers an investor would.

We manage single-family homes, condos, and small multifamily buildings, pairing responsive tenant service with clear reporting through our Buildium owner portal.

Unity Property Management is the first company in the growing UNITY family.
